**Idea:** every name is a person and every person a story — memory is how we stay connected to the people we love. Answers the hero question ("Searching for names a little more often lately?") with warmth, not worry. Concept asked for by the client: *meaningful connection*.

- Audience: Healthy & Wellness Individuals (active independent ager, persona M1) with family. Product not shown. No text in frame. No headscarves (client instruction).
- Format: 16:9, 1080p, 15 s, one continuous take, silent; plays once and holds its last frame on the web.

| Time | Beat | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Routine | Bright living room, morning window light; a wall of family photos. Grandmother (~68, short silver hair, navy linen shirt) carries a new framed photo; granddaughter (~7, light-blue hairband) skips beside her; the frame rests on the sideboard. |
| 3–6 s | Connection | The girl points at a photo; the grandmother touches it and tells its story (lips, hands). |
| 6–9 s | Clue → self-directed clarification | Next photo: a brief amused pause; she lifts the frame and checks the name handwritten on the back (illegible), nods, sets it straight; both laugh. |
| 9–12 s | Continuation | Together they hang the new frame — a photo of the two of them. |
| 12–15 s | Landing | Camera eases back: the two in front of a wall full of faces, her hand on the girl's shoulder; holds. Left third calm for the headline. |

Colour: navy shirt (anchor), blue vase and pale-blue frames (support), light-blue hairband (echo); white wall and pale oak dominate.
