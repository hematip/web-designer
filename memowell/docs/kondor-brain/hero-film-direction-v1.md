## VIDEO-SCENARIO-v2 (route A — "the name arrives")
1. **Routine** — a bright morning market under white shade sails; a woman of about 65 chooses lemons at a stall, a blue woven basket on her arm.
2. **Clue** — a friend of the same age calls to her from the next stall and walks over, smiling.
3. **Suspended action → searching eyeline** — she smiles back, a lemon still in her hand; her eyes drift up and aside for a beat, amused rather than worried.
4. **Retained agency** — the name comes; she says it (no sound) and steps forward.
5. **Continuation / landing** — a light embrace; the two walk on together along the stalls; the frame settles wide with a calm left third for the headline.

Scenario ledger: S01 market, morning · S02 woman ~65 · S03 choosing lemons · S04 blue basket · S05 friend same age · S06 friend calls and approaches · S07 lemon held (pause) · S08 eyes up/aside, amused · S09 says the name · S10 steps forward · S11 embrace · S12 walk on together · S13 wide landing, quiet left third. All mapped below.

⚠ **S09 (the name arriving)** — on a Memowell page a recall moment can read as an improved-memory outcome.
